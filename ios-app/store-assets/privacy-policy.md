# Privacy Policy — FES Community

_Last updated: 2026-05-07_

FES Community is an open-source community application. It is not operated by a company. This policy describes what the app does and does not do.

## Data we collect
**None.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no telemetry.

## Data stored on your device
- Your downloaded and self-created watchface files (in the app sandbox)
- App settings (locale, last-used watch identifier)

This data never leaves your device except when you explicitly send a watchface to your paired FES U Watch over Bluetooth.

## Network access
The app fetches a public, static catalog of watchfaces from a GitHub Pages mirror (`t3mr0i.github.io`). These requests contain only the file path; no identifying headers are added.

If you use the optional AI-powered watchface generator, the prompt you enter is sent to the OpenAI API. Image generation is performed by OpenAI under their terms.

## Permissions we request
- **Bluetooth** — to connect to your FES U Watch.
- **Photos / Camera** — only when you choose to import a photo into the watchface generator.

These permissions are used only for the stated purpose. Nothing is uploaded to a server we control.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages. We do not collect any data, including from children.

## Contact

## Changes
